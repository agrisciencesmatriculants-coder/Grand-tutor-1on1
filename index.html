VukaTV — South African Low-Data Learning Platform

VukaTV helps South African learners and university students learn more with less data: live telegraph lessons that stream slides and voice instead of heavy video, an AI study hub that turns notes into flashcards and quizzes, cheat-proof assessments that earn Vuka Points, and paid one-on-one varsity tutoring over video.

Subjects

Every official DBE subject for Grades 8 to 12 — all eleven official languages at Home, First Additional and Second Additional level, Mathematics, Mathematical Literacy, Technical Mathematics, Physical Sciences, Life Sciences, Accounting, Business Studies, Economics, Geography, History, Information Technology, Computer Applications Technology, Agricultural Sciences, Tourism, Consumer Studies, Hospitality Studies, Engineering Graphics and Design, the technologies, and the arts — plus South African university courses organised by faculty, degree, department and year of study.

Screened tutors

Every VukaTV tutor passes a seven-step screening: written motivation, verified ID and academic transcript, an intro video, and two 30-mark subject papers written in the style of official DBE and university past papers. Papers are marked by external AI examiners and reviewed by a human before any tutor teaches.

Safety and accountability

Accounts are verified by welcome code, a management team of doctoral-level AI managers oversees quality, complaints and learner success, and a proportionate security system protects members without punishing the innocent.
